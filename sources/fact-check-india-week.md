## Judgment calls

- **Butter chicken (dl4):** Moti Mahal's claim is disputed between rival restaurants, so the question says "is said to have been invented".
- **Kerala's name (kl12):** The "kera" (coconut) origin is popular but not settled; worded "often said to come from".
- **Snake boats (kl6):** Crews range from about 100 to over 120; the question asks "about how many".
- **Nepal (dl11):** The Nepal flag question and the Sikkim map question (kc5) were kept apart so neither gives the other away.
