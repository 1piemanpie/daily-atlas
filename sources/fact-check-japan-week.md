## Judgment calls

- **Greater Tokyo (tk9):** Estimates range from about 33 to 37 million depending on the boundary, so the answer is "over 30 million", and the card doesn't call it the world's biggest (a 2025 UN report ranked Jakarta first).
- **Prime minister (tk8):** A recent fact on purpose. If it goes stale before the week airs, the question still holds: she was the first woman in the job.
- **Taiwan (ok1):** Shown as its own shape on the map, named Taiwan, as on the world map.
- **Okinawa-day flag (ok8):** Asks for the giant neighbor's flag "today", since the old empire's flag was different.
- **Question ids:** Prefixes tk, ky, hd, ok, os, hr were checked against every earlier week.
