# USA week fact-check (2026-10-27)

I reviewed every question and fact card in the USA week, plus the placement quiz. The claims below were the ones most likely to be wrong or out of date, so I checked each against a source.

## Fixed

| Question | Was | Now | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Louisiana alligators | "roughly 2 million" | "more than 3 million" | [Louisiana Wildlife & Fisheries](https://wlf.louisiana.gov/page/alligator-management) |
| Smokies vs Grand Canyon | "over 12M, roughly double" | 12.2M vs 4.9M in 2024, nearly 2.5x | [Smithsonian, 2024 visits](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/these-were-the-most-and-least-visited-national-parks-in-2024-180986251/) |
| Mission Control fact | Called "Houston, Tranquility Base here" the first words spoken from the Moon | Armstrong's first call home after landing | Common knowledge correction (the first words after touchdown were "Contact light") |
| Red River border | "argued until the 2000s" | settled by a 2000 boundary compact | [Oklahoma Historical Society](https://www.okhistory.org/publications/enc/entry?entry=RE008) |
| Spanish place names | Monterey = "king's mountain" (it's named after a viceroy) | Fresno = "ash tree" | Replaced with a clearer example |
| Ukulele | "Madeiran immigrants" | "Cabinet makers from Madeira, a Portuguese island, arrived in 1879" | [TheCollector](https://www.thecollector.com/who-invented-the-ukulele/) |
