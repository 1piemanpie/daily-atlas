## Judgment calls

- **Xinjiang (xj7):** The fact card mentions the detention camps in one neutral sentence, citing the UN human rights office's 2022 assessment, since leaving them out would make the card misleading.
- **Sichuan's name (sz9):** Worded "popularly said to mean", with the historians' explanation on the card.
- **Hong Kong (day):** Counted as its own place in coverage (HKG), and pinned to the Hong Kong shape on the world map.
- **Bian lian (sz12):** "Breathe fire" is a real part of some Sichuan opera acts, but face-changing is the famous one the question asks about.
- **Question ids:** The Sichuan day uses `sz` because `sc` is already the Scotland day.
