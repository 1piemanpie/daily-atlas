## Judgment calls

- **Monarchs (gd3):** The butterfly reserve straddles Michoacán and the State of Mexico. The question says "Jalisco's neighbor", and only Michoacán borders Jalisco.
- **Mojito (hv8):** The Hemingway link is a bar's claim, so the fact card says historians doubt it.
- **Maya zero (yu4):** Worded "among the first", since the earliest use is debated.
- **Belize (jm6):** It was renamed from British Honduras in 1973 and became independent in 1981. The question asks about 1981.
