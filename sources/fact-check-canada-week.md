## Judgment calls

- **Spirit bears (bc6):** The share of white bears varies by island; the card says "about 1 in 10" for the region.
- **Saskatchewan's borders (ab1):** "All lines drawn by surveyors" is the usual way it's put; its east border has small jogs, but none follow rivers or coasts.
- **Quebec's walls (qc2):** Worded "the only one north of Mexico that still has most of its city walls", as UNESCO describes it.
- **Chinook temperature jump (ab12):** Records go higher; the card uses a safe "over 30 °F in an hour".
