## Judgment calls

- **St Patrick (be8):** He was born in "Roman Britain". Wales and Scotland were left out as options because both are claimed birthplaces inside Roman Britain.
- **Anglo-Saxons (wa3):** The answer is Germany. The Jutes came from Denmark, which the fact card says.
- **Westminster Abbey (lo12):** The question says "nearly every" coronation. Henry III's first crowning was at Gloucester.
